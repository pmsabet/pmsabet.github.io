About

I’m a UX designer finishing an M.S. in Information Science (HCI) at UNC Chapel Hill in December 2026. I’m looking for an entry-level UX design role.

I’m most interested in SaaS and enterprise software: the large, complex systems people use every day to get their work done. I like figuring out how those systems are organized and making them easier to understand and use.

I’m early in my career, and I’d like to work on a team where I can learn from more experienced designers, engineers, and product managers, and get more practice with complex systems.

About

I’m finishing my M.S. in Information Science (HCI) at UNC Chapel Hill in December 2026. I went back to school after years of parenting and working various jobs. I’d found a creative field built around what I’ve always been curious about: how people make sense of information, and how technology can work with the way they think instead of against it.

I came into UX just as AI tools were reshaping the field. I use them to design and prototype quickly, but I can still build everything manually in Figma. The same goes for research: AI tools can speed up parts of it, but understanding people is still human work.

Right now I’m looking for a junior or mid-level role on a team that values both design and research. SaaS, enterprise software, and AI platforms are where I’d most like to grow.

When I’m not iterating on a design problem, I’m usually on a long walk with friends or hanging out with my two daughters, ideally after a good cup of coffee. Most of life reads like sketch comedy to me, which helps with all of it.

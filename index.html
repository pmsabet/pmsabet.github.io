How I work

  1. 01

    Interview users

    I talk with the people who use the system to learn what they need and where they get stuck.

  2. 02

    Organize the content

    I sort what I learn and arrange the content so people can find what they’re looking for.

  3. 03

    Design and test the screens

    I design the screens in Figma, then run usability tests to see what works and what doesn’t.
